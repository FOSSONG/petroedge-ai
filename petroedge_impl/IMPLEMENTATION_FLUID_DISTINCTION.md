# Fluid distinction correction

This correction makes the water/oil/gas interpretation explicit and visible.

- Fluid probabilities are calculated per depth sample.
- A dominant-fluid depth track is displayed.
- Oil, gas, water and residual-hydrocarbon probability curves are displayed together.
- Every detected pay interval includes predominant fluid, confidence, oil/gas/water probabilities, mean porosity and mean water saturation.
- Overall fluid probabilities are based on pay samples when pay is present, rather than being diluted by the entire logged interval.
- The API now reports evidence availability and a low/moderate/high reliability grade.
- The interface clearly states that log-only fluid typing is screening and should be confirmed with pressure, PVT, mud-log or production evidence.
